THE COMPOST CYCLE

A living, breathing process — each step explained. Watch the cycle come alive.

1
Gather
Dry leaves & green waste collected from gardens and landscapes
2
Leaves placed in ventilated containers to begin natural breakdown
3
Decompose
Leaves rot into dark, crumbly organic matter rich in nutrients
4
Base Soil
Screened garden soil added for structure and microbial life
5
Mix
Rotted leaves blended thoroughly with soil using our secret ratio
6
Cure
Dark, earthy compost cured and ready for application & sale
7
Nourish
Compost applied to gardens & flowers — the cycle begins anew
